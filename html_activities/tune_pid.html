Cart-Pole PID Tuning

The pendulum starts tilted away from vertical. Your PID controller pushes the cart back and forth to bring the pole upright (θ = 0) and keep it there. Goal: tune the gains for the fastest settling time — the time after which |θ| stays inside a ±5% band of the initial tilt (reported below). Tips: raise P until it fights gravity (expect overshoot), add D to damp the overshoot, add I last for residual error. When you're happy with the response, transfer your gains to the robot — odrive_ctrl_v7.py uses negative gains, shown under each slider.
